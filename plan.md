# Nourish product roadmap

1. **Recipe discovery and nutrition tools — delivered.** Branded responsive home page, 420 illustrative meal combinations, searchable categories, recipe instructions, general embedded cooking videos, session saves, and working adult nutrition calculators.
2. **Editorial recipe library.** Have a qualified nutrition professional review portions, calorie and macro consistency, allergens, preparation times, and food-safety guidance. Replace generated combinations with individually tested recipes and matching licensed photos. Curate and verify an embeddable video for each recipe; provide captions and fallback written guidance.
3. **Personal recipe collection.** Add optional accounts and persistent favorites using Netlify Identity and Netlify Database, with a clear privacy policy and account deletion.
4. **Personal meal planning.** Add weekly plans, portions, grocery lists, and dietary exclusions. Introduce reviewed goal guidance and expanded measurement units after the core content is validated.
